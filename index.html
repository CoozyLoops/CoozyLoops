About us

Every loop has a little story

CozyLoops began with one ball of pink yarn, a borrowed hook and a lot of video tutorials. On busy days, crochet became my quiet corner, a calm way to slow down and make something with my own hands. Today that corner has grown into a tiny shop where I turn soft yarn into little things that make people smile.

With love, the maker behind CozyLoops ♡

How your piece is made

  1. Choosing the yarnI pick soft, skin-friendly yarns in gentle colours that feel as good as they look.
  2. Hooking it by handEvery stitch is made by hand, never by machine. A bag takes about ten hours; a bouquet can take two days.
  3. Checking every detailLoose ends are woven in, tassels are tightened and each piece is shaped until it sits just right.
  4. Packing with careYour order is wrapped in tissue paper with a handwritten thank-you note inside.

What CozyLoops stands for

  • Slow, not mass-made. No two pieces are exactly alike, and that is the charm.
  • Small batches. I make only what I can make well, so every order gets real attention.
  • Gifts that mean something. Flowers that never wilt and bunnies that last for years.
